# Episode 17 – Observing Agents

**Question:** *How do we observe agents as they reason, decide and act?*

Cloud-native observability explains the health and behaviour of distributed systems, but agents introduce additional questions about reasoning, decisions, tool use and long-running execution.

This episode explores the capabilities needed to make agent behaviour understandable, traceable and operationally observable.

- Architectural diagrams communicate architectural states. Focus canvases communicate evolving ideas.

If a scene represents the architecture itself, prefer generating a sequence of complete diagrams rather than revealing individual boxes and connectors. Reserve progressive reveals for focus canvases, conceptual decompositions, timelines, lists, and explanatory overlays.

- Every scene should communicate one architectural idea.

If communicating that idea requires more than four equally important visual elements, create additional scenes or diagram states.

Do not shrink or compress content simply to fit within a single frame.

The viewer's attention should always have an obvious focal point.

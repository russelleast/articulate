# Episode 0003 production plan

## Problem

Rebuild Episode 3 around the new presenter delivery without inheriting the former Companion-era timing or retrospective narrative.

## Principles

- Presenter recording is the timing authority.
- Begin with the architectural problem, not an AI-native label.
- Deterministic and intelligent implementations remain context-dependent alternatives.
- Architectural significance accumulates gradually through repeated choices and new responsibilities.
- The ending remains unresolved: AI-native architecture is the hypothesis the journal will test.

## Capabilities and visual structure

1. Presenter opening and restrained repository evidence for earlier experiments.
2. Problem → Principles → Capabilities → Architecture → Runtime → Technology.
3. Deterministic and LLM-assisted approaches shown as valid branches from the same problem.
4. Repeated intelligent choices gathering into architectural significance.
5. Prompt, model, evaluation, regression, observability and governance responsibilities.
6. Brief AI-powered versus AI-native distinction.
7. Intelligence beside persistence, messaging, security, reliability and observability at runtime.
8. Agents framed as an implementation pattern rather than the architecture.
9. Reflective unanswered questions and presenter-led hypothesis ending.

## Runtime and outputs

The presenter source is decoded once. Frame boundaries are derived from reviewed narration timestamps and resolved at 30 fps. Audio is normalised to -16 LUFS using EBU R128. The renderer produces a 1920×1080 H.264/AAC final, speech-derived SRT captions, deterministic review frames and contact sheets.

The publication thumbnail follows the refreshed presenter-led Episodes 0001–0002 treatment. It uses a reviewed frame from the new recording and a concise AI-native hypothesis message that remains readable at mobile size.

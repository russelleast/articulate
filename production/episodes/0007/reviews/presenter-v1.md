# Episode 0007 Presenter v1 Review

## Review intent

Confirm that the narrator remains the episode's anchor while each visual clarifies a relationship in the Knowledge Model rather than decorating the spoken track.

## Architectural checks

- Information is distinguished from connected, interpreted knowledge.
- Claims expose source, confidence, evidence and temporal status.
- Evidence can strengthen or challenge claims.
- The Knowledge Model is conceptual and is not equated with a graph.
- Discovery, conversation, reasoning, impact analysis and agents use the Knowledge API.
- Architectural Intelligence is framed as support for—not replacement of—architectural judgement.

## Visual checks

- Presenter framing and dark environment match the established series grammar.
- Diagrams build one idea at a time and remain legible at 1080p.
- Repository and Evidence scenes are brief and purposeful.
- No old floating Focus Canvas or new archetype appears.
- The thumbnail reads at 160×90.

## Publication checks

- Final video, SRT, thumbnail, diagram sources and SVGs are present.
- Automated production and render validation pass.
- Full-duration playback has been checked for audio/video synchronisation and transition pacing.

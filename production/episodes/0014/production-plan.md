## Intended outcome

The viewer should understand that new information becomes proposed knowledge rather than directly mutating accepted architectural knowledge, and that Once Was, As Is and To Be are temporal viewpoints over one connected knowledge model.

## Editorial treatment

The recorded presenter remains the episode's continuity. Seven restrained diagrams carry the relationships that benefit from spatial explanation. The Order Service database example persists from PostgreSQL through MongoDB to DynamoDB, so evolution is shown as accumulated architectural history rather than replacement.

## Review gates

- Source and narration: approved inputs
- Scene plan: phrase-aligned to recorded delivery
- Diagrams: deterministic SVGs with semantic D2 sources
- Rough cut: requires continuous playback and contact-sheet review
- Publication: requires video, thumbnail and subtitle validation

## Risks and mitigations

- Recognition errors: correct terminology against the recorded words.
- Dense diagrams: limit each diagram to one question and inspect at reduced size.
- Presenter discontinuity: preserve one continuous source clock and embedded audio.
- Baseline ambiguity: explicitly label viewpoints and keep them connected to one model.

No ADR is required. This episode applies the accepted media architecture.

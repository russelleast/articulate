# Episode 0002 Presenter Production Plan

Episode 0002 presents the vision that follows Episode 0001's problem statement. Russell's continuous direct-to-camera recording remains the human anchor. Visual interventions are reserved for concepts that benefit from structure: incomplete definitions, the distinction between artefacts and understanding, evolution of knowledge, the human/AI responsibility boundary, conversation as an interface and Architectural Intelligence.

## Production decisions

- Use the complete clean performance from 00:00.000 to 05:58.600; only unused trailing room tone is removed.
- Embedded audio remains continuous across visual scenes and is normalised to −16 LUFS, 7 LU LRA and −1.5 dBTP during final mux.
- Scene boundaries and timeline events resolve to integer frames at 30 fps.
- Presenter framing, soft-luminance edge treatment, typography, dark canvas and transition vocabulary follow Episode 0001 presenter refresh v1.
- Three enduring concepts are authored in shared D2 sources and rendered through the repository diagram pipeline.
- Captions are derived from the reviewed recording transcript, including spoken variations from `narrative.md`.

No ADR is required: this production applies the established presenter and deterministic-timeline architecture without changing the runtime contract.

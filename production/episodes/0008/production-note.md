The original fourteen-section cut was expanded to 53 narration-led scenes. Attention now moves from presenter to connected-domain diagram within the opening; through five messaging-platform reasoning states; from document RAG to relationship traversal; among retrieval strategies chosen by question type; through five visually distinct reasoning capabilities; into the Agentic RAG feedback loop and Knowledge Reasoning Layer; through the reasoning-before-agents progression; from the full knowledge graph to an extracted reasoning map; and back to the presenter for the conclusion.

## Engine limitations

The renderer treats an authored SVG as a static unit. Progressive explanation therefore cuts between complete notation states; it does not reveal or animate nodes inside one SVG. The shared contact-sheet workflow also depends on an assembled MP4, so an episode-local pre-render plate review was added to preserve the required editorial review gate. These limitations were documented rather than replaced with manually routed boxes or lower-quality connectors.
